## Trust and accessibility

- What increased trust?
- What would make the participant stop trusting the product?
- What interaction/content was inaccessible, exhausting, or unclear?
- Did the participant interpret any estimate as a guarantee or credential?

## Facilitator synthesis

Separate observation from interpretation. Do not infer ability, motivation, or demographic traits that were not directly relevant and consented.
