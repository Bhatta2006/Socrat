## Consent

Before the session, the facilitator reads and records each choice:

1. I am at least 18 and voluntarily agree to participate. `Agree / Do not agree`
2. I understand participation is unpaid and I may skip a question or stop at any time. `Acknowledged / Not acknowledged`
3. I agree that Socrat may retain written notes for product research for 30 days after the pilot finishes, as described below. `Agree / Do not agree`
4. I understand there will be no audio, video, or screen recording. `Acknowledged / Not acknowledged`
5. I separately agree to be contacted about an optional follow-up pilot during that retention period. `Agree / Do not agree`

Without adult participation consent, stop. Without notes consent, do not retain research answers or count the session toward the pilot evidence; do not pressure the participant to agree. Refusing optional follow-up does not exclude an otherwise eligible participant. Record participant ID, consent choices, date, and notice version privately.
