# M0 pilot recruitment and consent template

**Owner must replace bracketed operational fields before use. Do not recruit until privacy review approves this notice.**

## Screener

1. Are you 18 years of age or older? `Yes / No`  
   A `No` ends recruitment without collecting further information.
2. Which goal best matches something you are actively pursuing?
   - Start programming/DSA from zero
   - Prepare for coding interviews
   - Improve competitive programming
3. Which language would you use? `Python / C++ / Java / Other`
4. Describe one DSA learning or practice activity you did in the last 30 days. A complete beginner may answer “none yet” and describe what prompted them to start.
5. What is your current experience in the selected language? `None / Syntax only / Solved problems / Professional use`
6. Do you use or want any accessibility accommodation for online learning/coding? `Optional free text / Prefer not to say`
7. Can you join one remote/in-person 60–75 minute session during `[window]`? `Yes / No`

Collect contact details only after a person qualifies. Store the screener ID separately from contact details.

## Invitation

You are invited to evaluate an unfinished learning-product prototype from Socrat. The session studies whether the goal-setting, planning, help, and progress concepts make sense. It is not a test of you, not a job interview, and not a validated educational assessment.

- Duration: 60–75 minutes
- Format: `[remote/in-person details]`
- Thank-you: `[compensation, if any]`
- Data controller/contact: `[entity and contact]`
- Research contact: `[name and contact]`

## Consent

Before the session, the facilitator reads and records each choice:

1. I am at least 18 and voluntarily agree to participate. `Agree / Do not agree`
2. I understand I may skip a question or stop at any time without losing agreed compensation. `Agree / Do not agree`
3. I agree that Socrat may retain redacted notes for product research until `[date/period]`. `Agree / Do not agree`
4. I separately agree to audio/screen recording until `[deletion date/period]`. `Agree / Do not agree`
5. I separately agree to be contacted about the 14-day follow-up pilot. `Agree / Do not agree`

Participation requires only item 1. A refusal of recording or follow-up does not exclude an otherwise eligible participant.

## Privacy notice minimum

Before use, attach an approved notice stating:

- who controls the data and how to contact them;
- exact purposes and legal basis/consent mechanism applicable to launch jurisdiction;
- categories collected and recipients/processors;
- storage region and retention/deletion timing;
- participant rights and withdrawal process;
- whether any automated decision materially affects participation (it must not in M0);
- incident/contact process.
