# M0 private review record template

**Status:** Blank template; no approval recorded.

**Handling:** Copy outside the repository before filling in identities.

Reviewer names need not be published. Actual identities and authentic decisions must be recorded privately and accessible to authorized gate reviewers. An anonymous promise or AI-generated approval cannot pass the gate.

## Record separately for each role

- Opaque record ID, role, actual reviewer name, relevant responsibility, and review date.
- Exact document, contract, and prototype versions reviewed.
- Decision: approve, changes required, or reject.
- Findings, accepted risks, responsible people, due dates, and required retest evidence.
- Reviewer-authenticated approval evidence and its private location.

## Required review scope

| Role | Decision |
|---|---|
| Product | Pilot result, V1 scope/exclusions, goals, metrics, and go/change/stop decision |
| Engineering | Architecture decisions, feasibility, policy scenarios, delivery risks, and technical risk ownership |
| Learning Design | Content/assessment validity, assisted/independent evidence, policy definitions, and pilot synthesis |
| Security/Privacy | Notice before recruitment, entity/contact, storage/access/deletion, threat model, and critical/high risk ownership |
| Design/Accessibility | Goal/next-action comprehension, accommodations, critical journey usability, and synthesis |

If one person covers several roles, record their responsibility and approval separately for each. The owner's scope confirmation does not approve every review area.

## Repository-safe summary

Only copy role, opaque record ID, reviewed version, decision, date, and redacted unresolved findings into the public gate/synthesis. Keep identities and authenticated evidence in the private register with agreed access and retention. No approvals have been supplied as of 2 October 2026.
